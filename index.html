Methodology & Data Sources

Duration: Calculated as the modified duration of a hypothetical 10-year Treasury bond with semiannual coupons, face value of 100, and coupon rate equal to the observed 10-year Treasury yield. The bond is assumed to trade at par. This is a model-based duration estimate, not the duration of one specific Treasury security held through time.

Term Premium: New York Fed ACM 10-year term premium estimates.

Market Analysis: Term Premium Change = (End - Start) x 100 basis points. S&P 500 Return = (End / Start - 1) x 100%.

Data sources: New York Fed ACM | Treasury Yield | S&P 500
