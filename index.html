Methodology

Duration is calculated for a hypothetical 10-year, semiannual-coupon Treasury bond issued at par. The annual coupon rate is set equal to the observed 10-year Treasury yield on each observation date. For 20 semiannual periods, the present-value-weighted average cash-flow time gives Macaulay duration. Modified duration equals Macaulay duration divided by (1 + y/2), where y is the annual yield expressed as a decimal.

This provides a consistent model-based duration estimate across historical dates. It is not the observed duration of a specific outstanding Treasury security.

Term premium measures the compensation investors require for bearing interest-rate risk. ACM estimates are based on the Federal Reserve Bank of New York's model. If ACM data cannot be retrieved, the dashboard attempts to use the alternative Kim-Wright estimate, with the source label changed accordingly.

The market comparison is descriptive and does not establish that a change in the term premium caused an equity-market move.
